## Program access limits (important)
- Bilt: current restaurant list is ONLY in the Bilt app / logged-in website Neighborhood > Dining tab. Bilt terms (updated 2026-10-27) state: "Bilt Members can access the current list of Neighborhood Dining restaurants in the Neighborhood Dining tab of the Bilt app." No public enumerable list exists.
- Rakuten Dining: offers are ONLY in the Rakuten app (In-Store > Dining), per Rakuten's own FAQ. Flat 5% cash back (or 5x Amex MR / Bilt points if chosen as payout), on full bill incl. tax/tip/drinks, dine-in only.
- Both are partly/wholly powered by Rewards Network. Bilt terms: "Certain Bilt Neighborhood Dining restaurants are also part of Rewards Network" + an "Enable Rewards" toggle for RN restaurants. DinePerq (June 2026) confirms Rakuten Dining is a Rewards Network program and Bilt Dining is partly RN-powered. A card can be in only ONE Rewards Network program at a time.
- Public Rewards Network partner sites (AAdvantage Dining, Marriott Eat Around Town) are JS apps; their search results are partially indexed (Eat Around Town shows 1,880 results within 30 mi of NYC) but individual merchant pages could only be verified for a subset via search indexing.
